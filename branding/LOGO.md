# FocusFlow logo

Created using the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool with a transparent background.
The original generated master is `focusflow-mark.png`. Android assets preserve its
alpha, center the visible mark, and scale it into adaptive-icon safe bounds.
Notification variants use the same silhouette in white at each Android density.

The flowing F pairs soft white and mint with forest green (#153D2E). It appears in
the launcher, Android themed icons, Android system launch screen, and notification
status icons. The in-app splash retains the original animated play emblem and
loading arc.

## Generation prompt

Create ONE polished final logo symbol for FocusFlow, a native Android focus timer and study productivity app. This is a production app icon foreground asset, not a presentation or mockup. Square canvas, genuine transparent alpha background. A distinctive, beautifully balanced abstract capital F made from two broad flowing ribbon-like strokes with rounded ends and clean intentional negative space; a subtle forward/upward direction conveys flow and focused progress. Calm, modern, minimal geometric brand design with exceptionally crisp flat vector-style edges. Use only soft white #F4FFF8 and fresh mint #74C69D for the two strokes, designed to be highly legible on solid deep forest green #153D2E. No dark strokes. No gradients, shadows, glow, glass, texture, 3D, clocks, circles, leaves, sparkles, text, words, letters beside the symbol, border, square tile, or surrounding decoration. The single F-shaped mark itself is centered precisely, occupying about 54 percent of the square canvas width and height, with generous equal transparent margin on all four sides for Android adaptive icon safety. Thick enough geometry to read clearly as a 24px launcher icon. Refined and original optical balance. Output only the final transparent mark.
